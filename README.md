Govern AI responsibly: risk registers, model inventories, framework mapping (NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001, EU AI Act), impact assessments, and control-tracking cycles at the analyst tier.

> **Status**: bootstrap curriculum plan authored (2026-07-08). See [`CURRICULUM.md`](CURRICULUM.md) for the 10-module / 3-project scope, [`JOB_REQUIREMENTS.md`](JOB_REQUIREMENTS.md) for the requirements catalog and public references it is grounded in, and [`.aicg/curriculum-plan.json`](.aicg/curriculum-plan.json) for the machine-readable plan. Live posting evidence collection is deferred to the next autonomous research cycle — see the **Status** section of [`JOB_REQUIREMENTS.md`](JOB_REQUIREMENTS.md).

**Role level:** 15 (entry / early-career — AI Governance family). Higher-level Governance tracks ([`senior-ai-governance-architect-learning`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/senior-ai-governance-architect-learning), [`head-of-ai-governance-learning`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/head-of-ai-governance-learning), [`chief-ai-officer-learning`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/chief-ai-officer-learning)) inherit and extend this packet with architecture and leadership scope.
