# Labs for mod-107-reading-eval-and-audit-evidence (Reading Eval, Red-Team, and Audit Evidence into Governance Records)

Guided, step-by-step hands-on sessions. Labs complement the module's four exercises — the exercises drill authoring from scratch (ex 01 eval-row authoring on fresh reports, ex 02 vendor-card and RSP reading plus benchmark authoring, ex 03 review-packet composition, ex 04 delegation triage against short vignettes); the labs exercise the *refresh-cadence and revision* disciplines the exercises do not directly reach — a quarterly refresh walk on a pre-filed row set that has gone stale in three specific ways, and a re-benchmark against a newly-published vendor RSP revision. All labs are code-free — no Python or notebook environment is required.

| # | Lab | Focus | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | [Quarterly Refresh Walk on a Drifted MCDA Eval-Row Set](lab-01-quarterly-refresh-walk-on-a-drifted-mcda-row-set.md) | Chapters 05 / 09 / 10 convergence — take four pre-filed MCDA `EVAL-<year>-<sequence>` rows with deliberately-induced drift (a safety-eval row with system-prompt and application version drift + a passed next-due date, a red-team row with a deployed-but-not-re-tested hot-fix, a RAG-triad row with two un-reconciled retrieval-index refreshes, and a vendor-system-card row with a known-upcoming successor-identifier migration), walk the quarterly refresh cadence row-by-row, author three chapter-10 escalation entries (one Class 2, one Class 1, one Class 3), project the escalation register, and update the packet-reference pointer for the next release gate. | 2 h |
| 02 | [Benchmark Re-Run on a Vendor RSP Revision](lab-02-benchmark-re-run-on-a-vendor-rsp-revision.md) | Chapters 06 / 07 / 08 / 10 convergence — take the pre-filed `BENCH-2026-014` row from chapter 08's worked example and a newly-published (fictional, for the lab) Anthropic RSP revision v2026-10 that consolidates the autonomy tripwires and introduces an ASL-2.5 reclassification for tool-enabled deployments, re-walk the four axes, close the AI-disclosure gap that shipped before deadline, open one conditional threshold-alignment gap on the MCDA v4.0 function-calling roadmap item, route a chapter-10 Class-2 escalation to the product owner and the control-library owner, and record the forward-looking packet-reference distinction between MCDA v3.9 (no closure required) and v4.0 (Class-4 applies on non-closure). | 90 min |
