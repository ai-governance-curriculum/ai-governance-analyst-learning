# Labs for mod-108-controls-and-compliance-evidence (Controls, Compliance Evidence, and Third-Party AI Vendor Review)

Guided, step-by-step hands-on sessions against fixed fixtures. The labs sit beside — but do not duplicate — the five module exercises, which each drill *authoring from scratch*. These labs drill the specific *walking a pre-assembled artefact set mid-period* and *running an event-triggered re-review* workflows where chapter 02's four stale-evidence flag rules, chapter 03's four-verdict discipline, chapter 06's twenty-two-column vendor row and ten-step intake, chapter 08's ForHumanity / BABL / IAF-accreditation discipline, and chapter 09's four escalation classes converge into the hours of work the analyst does between cycles.

| # | Lab | Duration | Grounding |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | [Mid-Cycle Stale-Evidence Sweep on a Pre-Assembled Control-Record Snapshot](lab-01-mid-cycle-stale-evidence-sweep-on-a-control-record-snapshot.md) | 2 h | Chapters [02](../02-control-testing-cycle-and-evidence-artefacts.md), [03](../03-control-status-report-for-internal-audit.md), [09](../09-delegation-boundary-and-escalation-patterns.md) |
| 02 | [Event-Triggered Vendor-Row Re-Review on a Certification Lapse and a New Sub-Processor](lab-02-event-triggered-vendor-row-re-review.md) | 2 h | Chapters [06](../06-third-party-ai-vendor-and-model-intake.md), [08](../08-independent-audit-and-bias-auditor-credentialing-forhumanity-and-babl-ai.md), [09](../09-delegation-boundary-and-escalation-patterns.md) |

Each lab ships a fixed fixture (record snapshot, dated change list, related artefact identifiers), a numbered step sequence with expected per-step outputs, a deliverable structure, a checkpoint block, and a short *why this is a lab, not an exercise* note explaining the specific workflow convergence the lab exercises that the exercises do not.
