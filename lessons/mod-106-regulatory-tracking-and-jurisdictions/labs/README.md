# Labs for mod-106-regulatory-tracking-and-jurisdictions (Cross-Jurisdiction Regulatory Tracking)

Guided, step-by-step hands-on sessions. Labs complement the module's five exercises — the exercises drill the tracker schema (ex 01), EU and US-state crosswalks (ex 02), Article 22 flagging (ex 03), the sector overlays (ex 04), and the change-note shape in the general case (ex 05); the labs exercise the chapters the exercises do not reach directly (chapter 03 US federal patchwork and its dynamic EO overlay, chapter 05 UK and Canada trajectories, chapter 09 voluntary codes) and run integration disciplines — a cadence cycle, a status-field reconciliation, a deployer-side voluntary-code row — that single-chapter authoring cannot test. All labs are code-free — no Python or notebook environment is required.

| # | Lab | Focus | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | [US Federal Monthly Deep Read on a Stale Branch](lab-01-us-federal-monthly-deep-read-on-a-stale-branch.md) | Chapter 01 cadence drill against a pre-assembled US federal branch with six rows of deliberate staleness, including an EO row that is wrong across two administration transitions; refresh row-by-row, author a chapter 10 change-note for the one row with a material change, close the loop with a routed action list to the FedBidDraft system owner, carry a `<!-- needs-research: -->` marker on the Section 1557 injunction status per chapter 03 | 2 h |
| 02 | [UK and Canada Trajectory Reconciliation on a Drifted Branch](lab-02-uk-canada-trajectory-reconciliation.md) | Chapter 05 status-field discipline drill against a pre-assembled UK + Canada branch authored at two different points in time, with AIDA marked *in progress* and the UK horizontal statute marked *likely to pass*; reconcile each row's status honestly, add the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 row the earlier author missed, carry the AIDA trajectory and any UK horizontal-statute trajectory to a separate `horizon-scan.md` with the chapter 04 promotion rule | 90 min |
| 03 | [Voluntary-Code Tracker Row for the GPAI Code of Practice](lab-03-voluntary-code-row-for-the-gpai-code-of-practice.md) | Chapter 09 reading drill against a stub `REG-2026-EU-010` row and an inventory context in which the enterprise is a *deployer* of in-scope GPAI models rather than a provider; populate every column with both the primary (provider) and secondary (deployer with vendor adherence as evidence input) read, frame the adherence trade-off for the governance owner in a short memo, and reserve the next Code-revision change-note identifier per chapter 10 | 90 min |
