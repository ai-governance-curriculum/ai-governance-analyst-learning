# mod-103-risk-assessment-authoring: First-Draft AI Risk Assessment Authoring

> Scaffolded by `aicg org execute-plan`. Lecture chapters and exercise content are authored on subsequent autonomous cycles.

**Estimated effort:** 12 hours

## Learning objectives

- Walk a new AI use case through NIST AI RMF MAP sub-categories and produce the first-draft context / stakeholder / risk-source narrative
- Layer ISO/IEC 23894 risk-source categories and the NIST AI RMF Generative AI Profile risks onto the MAP output to produce a scored risk register entry
- Apply the US Federal Reserve SR 11-7 model-risk-management shape when the enterprise is a regulated financial institution
- Route risks to the appropriate owner (product, ML, security, legal, privacy) with the residual-risk claim and a proposed mitigation path — deferring harm-model methodology depth up to ai-risk-engineer and quantitative risk methodology depth up to senior-ai-governance-architect
- Distinguish inherent, residual, and control-defeated risk in a way an executive audience can act on
