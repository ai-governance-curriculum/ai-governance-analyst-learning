# Resources — mod-103 First-Draft AI Risk Assessment Authoring

## NIST AI RMF anchors

- [NIST AI RMF 1.0 (AI 100-1)](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.100-1.pdf) — MAP function; subcategory numbering.
- [NIST AI RMF Playbook](https://airc.nist.gov/AI_RMF_Knowledge_Base/Playbook) — suggested actions per MAP subcategory.
- [NIST AI RMF Generative AI Profile (AI 600-1)](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.600-1.pdf) — the 12 GenAI risks.
- [NIST AI Resource Center Knowledge Base](https://airc.nist.gov/AI_RMF_Knowledge_Base) — living hub.

## ISO/IEC risk-management standards

- [ISO/IEC 23894:2023 — AI Risk Management Guidance](https://www.iso.org/standard/77304.html) — AI-specific risk sources.
- [ISO 31000:2018 — Risk Management Guidelines](https://www.iso.org/standard/65694.html) — general risk-management base standard.
- [ISO/IEC 42001:2023 — AI Management System](https://www.iso.org/standard/81230.html) — Clause 6 planning; the standard that references 23894.
- [ISO Guide 73:2009 — Risk management vocabulary](https://www.iso.org/standard/44651.html) — shared terminology.

## Financial-services model risk

- [US Federal Reserve SR 11-7 — Guidance on Model Risk Management](https://www.federalreserve.gov/supervisionreg/srletters/sr1107.htm) — the canonical MRM guidance.
- [OCC Bulletin 2011-12](https://www.occ.gov/news-issuances/bulletins/2011/bulletin-2011-12.html) — OCC companion to SR 11-7.
- [OCC Bulletin 2020-10 on Model Risk Management](https://www.occ.gov/news-issuances/bulletins/2020/bulletin-2020-10.html) — third-party model considerations.
- [OCC Bulletin 2023-17 on Third-Party Relationships](https://www.occ.gov/news-issuances/bulletins/2023/bulletin-2023-17.html) — third-party risk guidance the AI overlay reaches for.
- [ECB Guide to Internal Models](https://www.bankingsupervision.europa.eu/) — EU banking-supervisor model guidance.
- [PRA SS1/23 — Model Risk Management Principles for Banks](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/prudential-regulation/publication/2023/may/model-risk-management-principles-for-banks-ss) — UK peer.
- [CFPB Circular 2022-03 on adverse action notification](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/compliance/circulars/circular-2022-03-adverse-action-notification-requirements-in-connection-with-credit-decisions-based-on-complex-algorithms/) — ECOA overlay for algorithmic credit decisions.

## What is deliberately not here

- Blog-post summaries of NIST AI RMF, SR 11-7, or ISO 23894 — read the primary text.
- Enterprise vendor risk-scoring calculators that produce a single aggregated score — analyst-tier discipline is to report the profile, not aggregate.
- Certification study guides.
