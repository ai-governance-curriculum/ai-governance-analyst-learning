# Labs for mod-101-ai-governance-landscape (AI Governance Landscape: Frameworks, Standards, and Values Baselines)

Labs are guided, step-by-step hands-on sessions. They complement — and do not duplicate — the module's `exercises/`.

| # | Lab | Focus | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | [One-Page Framework Cheatsheet for a Product Team](lab-01-one-page-framework-cheatsheet.md) | Author a single-page, stakeholder-facing cheatsheet distilling NIST AI RMF, NIST AI 600-1, ISO/IEC 42001, the EU AI Act, and the OECD/CoE/UNESCO baseline | 90 min |
| 02 | [End-to-End Intake Walk Across All Five Frameworks](lab-02-end-to-end-intake-walk.md) | Thread one fictional intake through EU AI Act tiering → NIST AI RMF MAP → GenAI Profile risks → ISO/IEC 42001 clause touchpoints → values-baseline check → deferral-map routing | 2 h |
| 03 | [Values-Baseline Audit of a Draft Responsible AI Policy](lab-03-values-baseline-policy-audit.md) | Produce an analyst-tier gap-finding memo against OECD, CoE, UNESCO, and the EU AI Act — flag only, do not rewrite | 90 min |
